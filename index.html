Dual-greenhouse digital twin

  1. Sensors and cameras in the two greenhouses are read by small local boards, and a programmable logic controller (PLC) switches the equipment.
  2. One site network carries all readings to the project laptop.
  3. On the laptop the readings pass through four steps: they are checked and stored, the twin model estimates the state and forecasts two hours ahead, a controller proposes setpoints within limits, and the interface sends approved setpoints back to the equipment.
  4. The Unity application shows the state of both greenhouses, and an operator can propose and approve a command there.
  5. The stored records are used offline to calibrate the model and to train and evaluate a controller. Only an approved controller returns to the decision step.
